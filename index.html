Source

Keep just the action: frames are captured only between the two trim thumbs. Drag the box edges on the video to crop out UI bars or distractions — changing either needs a replay.

Status

Result

Instagram sizes. In fill, drag the bright window on the picture to frame the subject; fit keeps the whole picture with blurred bands.

Controls

Spacing between the subject's copies. Higher = fewer, more spread-out copies. Updates the result instantly (no need to replay).

Detection sensitivity. Set automatically after each capture (the app analyzes the video and picks the best value); tweak to taste. Lower = catches more of the subject (and more noise); higher = stricter and cleaner.

Paints the detected subject in a solid color instead of its real pixels (pick the color on the right).

Save video shares the build-up clip — the silhouettes painting in one by one, as you just watched it compose.

Analysis — how the threshold was picked

Histogram of per-pixel difference scores against the background model (in sigmas, log scale), accumulated from sampled frames. Light bars = background noise, dark bars = subject; the dashed line is the cut Otsu's method picked.
